Heat Transfer Mechanisms

Modes of Heat Transfer

Thermal Conduction

Conduction is the transfer of energy from more energetic particles to less energetic particles as a result of interaction between particles.

Fourier's Law:

Q = -k A (dT/dx)

k = thermal conductivity (W/m.K)

Thermal Convection

Convection occurs between a solid surface and an adjacent fluid that is in motion.

Newton's Law of Cooling:

Q = h A (Ts - T∞)

h = convection heat transfer coefficient (W/m².K)

Thermal Radiation

Radiation is the energy emitted by matter in the form of electromagnetic waves.

Stefan-Boltzmann Law:

Q = ε σ A Ts⁴
